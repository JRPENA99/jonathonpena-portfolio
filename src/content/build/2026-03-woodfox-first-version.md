The first Woodfox site went up in early March. Plain HTML and CSS, one page, a hero video, product photos, an events section and a custom domain.

Looking back at the commit history is humbling: a dozen commits in a row adjusting the header logo. *Make logo larger. Center logo. Lower logo slightly.* That's what happens when there's no design system. Every decision gets made by nudging pixels.

**What worked:** it shipped, and the domain was live.

**What didn't:** every content change meant editing markup, and the copy didn't say much beyond "good coffee."

Both of those turned into the brief for the later rebuilds.
