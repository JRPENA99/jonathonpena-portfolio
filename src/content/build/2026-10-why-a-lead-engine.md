The Lead Engine comes from watching how B2B sales research actually happens: dozens of browser tabs, a spreadsheet, and the same questions every time.

- What does this company do?
- Do they import or export?
- Are they a fit for what we sell?
- Who's the right person?
- Who should I call first?

None of those questions is hard. Answering them for hundreds of companies is. And it gets redone for every new territory.

The workflow I'm designing around: **Discover → Enrich → Classify → Prioritize → Research → Outreach.**

The rule I keep coming back to: the engine has to show *why* an account surfaced. A score without a reason is just another number for a salesperson to ignore.

I built a [front-end prototype with sample data](/projects/lead-engine/) to think through the interface before the data pipeline is done.
