Woodfox Coffee moved a big step toward launch this week.

- **New home.** The site now lives at **woodfoxcoffee.com**.
- **Packaging concepts.** The drawn placeholder bags were replaced with three packaging mockups: Single Origin, House Blend No. 01 and Espresso Blend. They're clearly labeled **In development** and **Not on sale yet**, and the text on the bags isn't treated as a product spec.
- **Brand palette.** Forest, rust and cream from the business plan, with restrained copper accents and a serif wordmark that matches the packaging.
- **A plainer voice.** I rewrote the copy across every page: shorter sentences, specific details, fewer taglines, no new factual claims. "Coffee you can actually taste" replaced the more polished, more generic version.
- **Ordering off, on purpose.** The shop from the preview is now behind a config flag. No prices, cart or checkout until a payment provider is set up and approved.
- **Contact routing.** General inquiries and orders go to separate inboxes, and forms open the visitor's email app until a form service is connected.

The same week, the brand also got its first NFC cards: one for the owner and three shared event-team cards for markets. More in [the NFC cards project](/projects/nfc-cards/).
