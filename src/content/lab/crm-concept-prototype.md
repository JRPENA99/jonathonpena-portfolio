This was the first version of the [Independent CRM](/projects/crm/): a front-end-only concept for working out the workflow before writing any backend. A pipeline board, an account drawer with notes, and a task list.

It did its job. It made clear that the day, not the pipeline, should be the home screen. The real app opens on a **Today** view for that reason.

Kept here as a record of where the project started. Everything resets on reload, and all data is invented.
