An interface study for the [Sales Lead Engine](/projects/lead-engine/). The question: how do you show a ranked list of accounts in a way a salesperson will actually trust?

My answer so far: show the score, but always next to the reason. The detail panel leads with *why it surfaced* and shows how far the account is through research.

Sample data only. The real scoring model is still being designed.
