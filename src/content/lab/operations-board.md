Operations work is mostly about flow: how much is waiting, how much is moving, and what's stuck. This board tries to answer those three questions without scrolling.

- **Work in flight** as a proportional strip, so a bottleneck is visible as a wide segment.
- **Throughput** by day, switchable between weeks.
- **Exceptions** separated from normal tasks. Problems shouldn't hide in the to-do list.

Shaped by real operations experience with inventory audits, tool tracking and job documentation. The data itself is sample data.
