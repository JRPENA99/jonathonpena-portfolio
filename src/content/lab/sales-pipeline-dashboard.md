What a salesperson or manager needs at a glance: how much is in the pipeline, how it's trending, where deals are sitting, and what happened recently.

Things I was testing:

- **Stages as filters.** Clicking a stage filters the account list instead of opening another page.
- **One trend, not five.** A single pipeline-value line with a hover readout instead of a wall of charts.
- **Next step on every row.** An account without a next step is a deal that's quietly dying.

All numbers and companies are sample data.
